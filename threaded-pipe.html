Threaded Pipe

Threaded pipes are a type of pipe with threads cut into the ends, allowing them to be screwed together with other threaded pipes, fittings, or valves. These pipes are widely used in plumbing, gas lines, and various industrial applications for their ease of assembly and disassembly.

Application :
  • Plumbing: Used for transporting water, gas, and other fluids in residential and commercial buildings.
  • Industrial : Employed in various industrial processes, including chemical transport, oil and gas pipelines, and compressed air systems.
  • HVAC: Used in heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems for distributing water and refrigerants.
  • Fire Protection: For lightweight, high-strength assemblies.
