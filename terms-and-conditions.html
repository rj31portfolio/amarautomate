These terms describe the use of the Amar Automats website and the basis for enquiries about our automotive components, precision turned parts, and custom manufacturing services. Please read them before using the website or sending an enquiry.

For a specific purchase, the quotation and order terms agreed with you govern the commercial details. These website terms do not replace an existing written agreement or limit rights that cannot be excluded under applicable law.

01

Website use

Use this website for lawful purposes, including learning about our products and contacting our team. Do not attempt to disrupt the website, gain unauthorised access, or submit harmful, misleading, or unlawful material.

When sending an enquiry, provide accurate contact details and information about your requirements so we can respond appropriately.

02

Product information

Product photographs, catalog images, and descriptions provide a general overview of our range. Please confirm the specifications of the component you need with our team before ordering.

Materials, dimensions, tolerances, finishes, quantities, and suitability for your application should be checked against the agreed specification. A website image alone should not be used as a manufacturing drawing or a confirmation of compatibility.

03

Quotations & orders

A website enquiry or request for a quote does not by itself confirm an order. Product availability, production capacity, pricing, and order acceptance must be confirmed with Amar Automats.

Review the quotation and agreed order details carefully. Any quotation validity period, minimum order quantity, taxes, packaging, or additional charges should be specified in the relevant quotation or order confirmation.

04

Custom components & drawings

For made-to-specification components, provide the relevant drawings, dimensions, material requirements, tolerances, finishes, and inspection requirements. Confirm the correct drawing revision and any required sample approval before production.

Only submit drawings and other materials that you own or are authorised to share for the requested work. Changes to an approved specification may affect pricing and production schedules and should be agreed in writing.

05

Payment & delivery

Payment arrangements, any advance payment, dispatch details, freight charges, and delivery responsibilities are those agreed for your order. Please refer to your quotation, invoice, and order confirmation for the applicable details.

Confirm required delivery dates with our team. Estimated lead times may depend on specification approval, material availability, manufacturing requirements, and transport arrangements. Contact us if your project has a critical deadline.

06

Changes, cancellations & quality concerns

Contact us promptly if you need to change or cancel an order. The available options depend on the agreed order terms and whether materials have been procured or manufacturing has started, particularly for custom components.

If you receive a damaged, incorrect, or potentially non-conforming component, share your order reference, a description of the issue, and supporting photographs or inspection information. Any return, replacement, refund, or other resolution will be assessed against the agreed specification, order terms, and applicable law.
