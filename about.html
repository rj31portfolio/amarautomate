About Amar Automats

Making available Automotive Small Components such as Special Insert Bolt, Light Insert Bolt, Nuts, Bolts, Screws and Hang Nuts that are known for their resistivity to wear & tear, rustproof nature, tough construction and dimensional perfectness...

Amar Automats is a formidable company, catering to the requirements of automotive industry via its Automotive Small Components, Modular lights & Domestic Lights components. Some of the widely demanded products of us are Precision Turned Nuts, Bolts, Screws and Hang Nuts. Being an ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer and supplier, we make sure to develop these from superior grade raw material as per industry stated norms.
