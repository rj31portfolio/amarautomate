01

Information you choose to share

The website contact form asks for your name, email address, subject, and message. If you contact us by telephone or WhatsApp, you may also share your phone number, profile details, and the information included in your conversation.

Your enquiry may include company details, quantities, product specifications, or drawings. Share only the information needed for your request and only information you are authorised to provide. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or identity documents through a general enquiry.

02

How enquiry information is used

Information received from you is used to understand your requirements, respond to questions, prepare quotations, and communicate about related products or orders. Where a business relationship follows, relevant correspondence may form part of the order and support records.

Technical information may also be processed by the website host and external providers to deliver their services, diagnose errors, and help protect their systems. This may include an IP address, browser information, request time, and the resource requested.

03

Third-party services & external links

This website loads Google Fonts and resources from content delivery networks, including unpkg and code.jquery.com. The contact page embeds Google Maps, and some pages link to or load externally hosted video. Loading these resources sends a request from your browser to the relevant provider.

WhatsApp links open a separate communication service. External websites and platforms apply their own privacy practices and may process information outside India. Review their policies before using them or sharing personal information.

Google Privacy Policy WhatsApp Privacy Policy
04

Cookies & browser data

External services such as embedded maps may use cookies or similar technologies according to their settings and policies. Browser caching and technical requests may also occur when the website loads images, fonts, styles, or scripts.

You can use your browser settings to review or delete site data, restrict third-party cookies, and control permissions. Blocking external resources may affect maps, media, fonts, or other website features. These browser controls do not delete information already shared in a call or message.

05

Sharing & retention

Handling an enquiry or order may involve the people responsible for responding to you and service providers supporting communications, hosting, or fulfilment. Information may also need to be disclosed where required by applicable law or in connection with a lawful request.

The relevant retention period depends on the type of information, the enquiry or order, and any record-keeping obligations. Contact our team to ask about records relating to you or to request deletion. Records that must be retained for legal or ongoing business obligations may not be eligible for immediate deletion.

06

Your choices & privacy requests

You can browse the product range without sending an enquiry. If you choose to contact us, limit your message to the details needed for your request.

You may contact us to ask about information you have shared, request a correction or deletion, or ask us to stop optional follow-up communications. If processing relies on your consent, you may ask to withdraw it. Requests are considered under applicable law and may require proportionate identity verification before information is disclosed or changed.

Call +91 96508 73232 and explain that your question concerns privacy. Provide enough context to identify the relevant enquiry without sharing unnecessary sensitive information.

07

Security & children

No website, electronic message, or storage system can guarantee absolute security. Avoid including confidential or sensitive personal information in a general enquiry, and contact us first if your project requires a specific arrangement for sharing confidential documents.

This website presents industrial products and business services and is intended for business enquiries, rather than use by children. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact our team so the matter can be reviewed.
